## Behind the Photo

This was one of my first photos I created with a screen background. I searched for an image of a dessert sunset and put this Star Wars Black Series Mandalorian action figure in front and the phot came out great.

I used a dark room with a small portable light to give the effect that The Mandalorian is walking through a dessert while the sun is setting. This is one of the first times I used lighting, but I love the way it turned out. I especially enjoyed this new figure because of the soft cape that wonderful details like the holes which resembled wear and tear.
