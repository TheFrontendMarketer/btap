## Behind the Photo

Spider-Punk was one of my favorite characters in Across the Spider-Verse and I love this Marvel Legends figure. My inspiration for this shot came from the lead singer of Rancid, Tim Armstrong. This was also one of the first times I used lighting and before I knew how to completely crop out the stand.

I like the way this came out and I feel like this expresses the energy of playing live music I was looking for.
